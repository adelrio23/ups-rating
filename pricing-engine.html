Rate Cards

Upload carrier rate Excel files (.xlsx, .xlsm). Auto-detects zone/weight layout.

Supported: Any layout where a row contains "Zone 2"–"Zone 8" (or UPS-style "Zone 132"–"Zone 138", "Zone 102"–"Zone 108", etc.) and col A is weight. Works with UPS, FedEx, multi-sheet workbooks. A parse report shows exactly how each sheet was read.

Customer Invoice

Upload carrier invoice .xlsx / .xlsm. Auto-detects FedEx, UPS, and custom layouts.

Auto-detected fields: Weight, Zone, Service, Base Charge, Discount, Misc/Fuel, Net Charge, Tracking. A parse report shows which columns were mapped and includes sample rows so you can verify.
